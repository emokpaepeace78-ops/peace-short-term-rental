# The Haven Apartments: Guest Support Playbook

A guest support playbook for a two bedroom short term rental in Lagos, Nigeria. It shows how I handle guest messages from the first reply to a closed ticket, who gets called when something goes wrong, and what a good response actually sounds like.

**View the live page:** https://emokpaepeace78-ops.github.io/peace-short-term-rental/

## What's inside

**Guest support workflow.** Eight steps I follow every time, from reading the message and checking the reservation to confirming the fix and documenting the ticket. Issues are sorted into Urgent, High and Normal, each with a first reply target of 10 minutes, 30 minutes and 2 hours.

**Escalation matrix.** Eleven common issues, such as a lockout, a water leak, no WiFi or a power outage, with the first action support takes and who takes over if that isn't enough. The rule behind it: don't just pass the problem on. Record it, tell the guest what is happening and follow up until it is closed.

**Five guest scenarios.** Each one is written out message by message, including the internal note to the property manager and the final ticket record.

1. WiFi not working
2. Locked out at night
3. Water leak in the bathroom
4. Unclean on arrival with a refund request
5. Power outage with the generator down

## Who it's for

Property owners, hosts and short term rental managers who want guest messages handled calmly, quickly and consistently, so guests feel looked after and problems don't pile up.
