Part one

Guest support workflow

Eight steps, the same every time. Whether the message is about a WiFi password or a flooded bathroom, the order does not change. Only the speed does.

  1. 1

    Receive the guest message

    Read it once, properly, and pin down five things.

    • Who is contacting us
    • Which property they are in
    • What the issue is
    • How urgent it is
    • Whether it affects their stay
  2. 2

    Check the reservation

    Verify the details before replying, so the guest never has to explain themselves twice.

    • Guest name
    • Check in and check out dates
    • Property
    • Booking status
    • Number of guests
    • Previous messages
    • Property instructions
  3. 3

    Assess priority

    Every issue gets one of three labels, and the label decides how fast everything else moves.

    Urgent

    Safety concerns, lockout, major water leak, serious property damage.

    First reply within 10 minutes
    High

    No WiFi, AC failure, plumbing problems, power outage, missing essential items.

    First reply within 30 minutes
    Normal

    General questions, amenity information, checkout questions, local recommendations.

    First reply within 2 hours
  4. 4

    Respond

    Acknowledge the concern, take ownership, and give a clear next step. Never make the guest repeat information they have already provided.

  5. 5

    Resolve or escalate

    If it sits within support guidelines, fix it directly. If someone else needs to step in, pass it to the right property manager, cleaner, maintenance provider or other contact.

  6. 6

    Keep the guest updated

    If the fix takes time, say so and keep checking in. A guest who hears nothing assumes nothing is happening.

  7. 7

    Confirm resolution

    Once action has been taken, go back to the guest and make sure the problem is actually gone.

  8. 8

    Document and close

    Every ticket ends with a record.

    • Issue
    • Action taken
    • Person contacted
    • Resolution
    • Follow up required

Escalation principle

Don't simply pass the problem to someone else. Record the escalation, tell the guest what is happening and follow up until the issue is closed.

Scenario 1 of 5

WiFi not working

Priority: High

A guest has just arrived and needs to work tonight. Basic troubleshooting first, escalation only when it fails.

Checked firstSarah, 2 guests, checked in today, booking confirmed, no earlier messages.

Guest · 6:42 PM · Airbnb

Hi, we've just arrived and the WiFi isn't working. I need to work tonight. Can someone please help?

Guest support · 6:44 PM

Hi Sarah, I'm sorry about that, especially since you need the WiFi for work tonight.

I'll help you get this sorted. Could you please check that the router is powered on and let me know what lights you can see? If it's easier, you can send me a quick photo of the router.

I'll also check the connection from our side and keep you updated while we work on it.

Guest · 6:49 PM

Just sent a photo. The power light is on but the internet light is red.

Guest support · 6:51 PM

Thanks Sarah, that helps. A red internet light usually means the router is on but isn't getting a signal from the provider. Please unplug it, wait 30 seconds, then plug it back in. Give it two minutes and tell me what the light does.

Guest · 6:58 PM

Restarted it. Still red.

Internal · 7:00 PM · To property manager

WiFi down at The Haven. Guest Sarah, checked in today, needs it for work tonight. Router powers on, internet light stays red after a restart (photo attached). Please contact the internet provider or advise on a backup. I've told her she'll hear from me by 7:30 PM.

Guest support · 7:01 PM

Sarah, thank you for trying that. The restart didn't clear it, so I've passed it to our property manager to contact the internet provider. I'll message you by 7:30 PM with where we are, even if it isn't fixed yet.

Guest support · 7:30 PM

Quick update: the provider has confirmed a fault on the line and an engineer is working on it. I'm staying on this and will check in again by 8:00 PM.

Guest support · 7:52 PM

Good news, Sarah. Our property manager confirms the line is back. Could you check that your devices have reconnected?

Guest · 7:55 PM

Yes, it's working now. Thank you so much!

Guest support · 7:56 PM

Wonderful, I'm glad you can get on with your work. If anything drops again tonight, message me straight away.

Ticket record · Closed
Issue
WiFi down on arrival, guest needs it for work.
Action taken
Guided guest through a router light check and restart, then escalated when the restart failed.
Person contacted
Property manager, who contacted the internet provider.
Resolution
Line restored at about 7:50 PM. Guest confirmed devices reconnected.
Follow up
Ask the provider for the cause. Add the router light check to the property guide.
What this shows

Support tries the simple fix first, hands over with a full summary when it fails, and gives the guest a time they will hear back by.

Scenario 2 of 5

Locked out at night

Priority: Urgent

Guests are standing outside with luggage at 10:48 PM. Speed matters, but so does checking who is asking for access.

Checked firstDaniel, 2 guests, arriving tonight, booking confirmed and name matches. Self check in with a door code.

Guest · 10:48 PM · WhatsApp

Hi, we're outside the apartment and the door code isn't opening. We have our luggage and it's late. Please help!

Guest support · 10:49 PM

Hi Daniel, I'm sorry, let's get you inside quickly. First, are you and your guest safe and somewhere comfortable to wait? Then please try the code once more, entering each digit slowly, and tell me what the lock does. I'm contacting our access contact now and I'll message you within 10 minutes either way.

Guest · 10:52 PM

We're fine, waiting in the lobby. Tried again and a red light flashes.

Internal · 10:53 PM · To emergency access contact and property manager

LOCKOUT at The Haven. Guest Daniel plus one, arrived 10:45 PM. Booking and name verified. Door code rejected, lock flashes red on retry. Guests are waiting safely in the lobby with luggage. Need someone with access as soon as possible. Please confirm your ETA.

Guest support · 10:56 PM

Thank you, Daniel. Our access contact is on the way and expects to reach you in about 15 minutes. I'll stay in this chat until you're inside.

Guest support · 11:12 PM

Our contact has just arrived at the building and is heading to your door. Let me know once you're in.

Guest · 11:20 PM

We're in. Thank you!

Guest support · 11:21 PM

So glad to hear it. Settle in and get some rest, and message me if you need anything tonight. I'll make sure the lock gets looked at so this doesn't happen again.

Next morning
Guest support · 9:00 AM

Good morning Daniel. Just checking you slept well and that the door is working properly for you today.

Ticket record · Closed
Issue
Guests locked out on arrival, door code rejected.
Action taken
Confirmed the booking before arranging access. Checked the guests were safe. Updated them every few minutes until they were inside.
Person contacted
Emergency access contact and property manager.
Resolution
Guests inside by 11:20 PM.
Follow up
Property manager to check the lock batteries and reset the code before the next check in. Morning check in message sent.
What this shows

Safety comes first, access is only arranged after the booking is verified, and the guest is never left wondering what is happening.

Scenario 3 of 5

Water leak in the bathroom

Priority: Urgent

Water is spreading across the floor. The guest needs clear safety instructions before anything else.

Checked firstTunde, 3 guests, night 2 of 4, booking confirmed, no earlier maintenance issues logged.

Guest · 2:15 PM · Airbnb

There's water coming from under the bathroom sink and it's spreading across the floor. What do we do?

Guest support · 2:16 PM

Hi Tunde, thank you for telling me straight away. Please keep everyone clear of the wet area and don't touch any sockets or switches near the water. If any bags are on the floor, move them somewhere dry. I'm sending emergency maintenance to you now. Could you send one photo from the doorway so I can brief them?

Guest · 2:19 PM

Sent. It's coming from the pipe under the sink and it's getting worse.

Internal · 2:20 PM · To property manager and emergency maintenance

URGENT water leak at The Haven. Guest Tunde, 3 guests in the apartment. Water from the pipe under the bathroom sink is spreading across the floor (photo attached). Guests told to keep clear and avoid sockets near the water. Need a plumber on site now. Please confirm your ETA.

Guest support · 2:24 PM

Thanks Tunde. Maintenance is on the way and should reach you by 3:00 PM. Please keep the bathroom closed off until they arrive. If the water gets near any sockets or you ever feel unsafe, step outside and tell me straight away.

Guest support · 3:05 PM

The plumber has arrived and is working on the pipe now. I'll check back with you once it's done.

Guest support · 3:50 PM

The pipe has been repaired and our cleaner is drying the floor. Is the bathroom usable for you now? I've also noted the incident for our property manager, who will contact you if anything more needs to be sorted for your stay.

Guest · 3:55 PM

All good now. Thanks for being so quick.

Ticket record · Closed
Issue
Major water leak from the pipe under the bathroom sink.
Action taken
Told guests to stay clear of the water and electrics. Requested a photo to brief maintenance. Kept guests updated at each stage.
Person contacted
Property manager, emergency maintenance, cleaner.
Resolution
Pipe repaired by about 3:45 PM and floor dried. Guest confirmed the bathroom is usable.
Follow up
Property manager to decide on any goodwill gesture. Inspect the other pipes at the next turnover.
What this shows

In an emergency the reply is short and gives the guest something to do. Anything about compensation is left to the manager, not promised by support.

Scenario 4 of 5

Unclean on arrival, refund requested

Priority: High

An unhappy guest asks for money back. Support fixes what it can, collects evidence and passes the refund decision to the right person.

Checked firstEmily, 2 guests, night 1 of 3, booking confirmed, refund policy reviewed. Cleaning notes show the unit was marked complete at 11:30 AM.

Guest · 4:10 PM · Airbnb

I'm really disappointed. The apartment wasn't clean when we arrived. The bathroom hadn't been cleaned and there's dust all over the bedroom floor. I want a refund for tonight.

Guest support · 4:12 PM

Hi Emily, I'm sorry. That isn't the welcome we want you to have, and I understand why you're frustrated. I want to put this right. Could you send me a couple of photos of the areas that weren't clean so I can pass them straight to the team? I'm also arranging for our cleaner to come back, and I'll confirm a time shortly.

Internal · 4:20 PM · To cleaner and property manager

Cleaning complaint at The Haven. Guest Emily arrived today and reports the bathroom not cleaned and dust on the bedroom floor (photos attached). Cleaning notes show complete at 11:30 AM. Cleaner: please return today and confirm a time. Manager: the guest has asked for a refund for tonight. That is outside my authority, so I need your decision. Booking is 3 nights and the standard refund policy applies. Guest is waiting for an answer.

Guest support · 4:25 PM

Emily, our cleaner can be with you at 5:30 PM, or at a time that suits you if you'd rather be out. On the refund, I've shared your request and photos with our property manager, who makes those decisions. I can't promise the outcome yet, but it is with the right person and I'll come back to you by 6:00 PM with an answer.

Guest support · 6:00 PM

Emily, our property manager has reviewed your photos and approved a partial refund for tonight. It will show in your Airbnb account, and the timing depends on Airbnb's processing. Once again, I'm sorry for the poor start.

After the cleaner's visit
Guest support · 7:15 PM

Is the apartment how it should be now? If anything is still not right, tell me and I'll deal with it tonight.

Guest · 7:20 PM

Yes, much better. Thanks for sorting it out.

Ticket record · Closed
Issue
Cleanliness complaint on arrival with a refund request for night 1.
Action taken
Apologised, collected photos, booked a return clean, and passed the refund request with a full summary to the manager.
Person contacted
Cleaner and property manager.
Resolution
Cleaner returned at 5:30 PM. Partial refund approved by the manager. Guest confirmed the apartment was clean.
Follow up
Property manager to review the cleaner's checklist for this turnover. Add photo proof of the bathroom to the cleaning sign off.
What this shows

Knowing the limit of your authority. Support never promises a refund it cannot approve, but gives the guest an honest timeline and a decision by a set time.

Scenario 5 of 5

Power outage, generator down

Priority: High

A Lagos evening in the heat with no lights and no AC. Support works out whether it is the area or the apartment before sending anyone out.

Checked firstJames, 3 guests, night 1 of 5, booking confirmed. Property guide includes backup power steps.

Guest · 8:05 PM · WhatsApp

The lights just went off and the AC has stopped. Is there a generator or backup?

Guest support · 8:07 PM

Hi James, sorry about that, I know it's uncomfortable in this heat. Let's get the lights back on. Is the whole apartment out or only some rooms? And can you see if the corridor or neighbouring flats have power? That tells me whether it's a wider outage or something inside the apartment. I'm sending you the backup power steps from our property guide now.

Guest · 8:10 PM

Whole flat is out. Corridor too, and the neighbours are dark.

Guest support · 8:12 PM

Thanks, that points to an outage on the area's supply and not a fault in the apartment. Please follow the backup power steps I sent and tell me if anything doesn't respond.

Guest · 8:18 PM

Followed them. The generator isn't coming on.

Internal · 8:20 PM · To property manager and generator technician

Power outage at The Haven, area wide (corridor and neighbours also out). Guest James, 3 guests. Backup generator not starting after the guide steps were followed. Please check fuel and the starter, and confirm your ETA.

Guest support · 8:22 PM

Understood, James. I've sent this to our property manager and generator technician. In the meantime, keep the fridge and freezer closed so they stay cold, and use your phone torch for light. I'll update you by 8:45 PM.

Guest support · 8:48 PM

The technician found the generator was low on fuel. It has been refilled and is starting now. Please let me know when the lights and AC are back.

Guest · 8:52 PM

Lights are on and the AC is running. Thanks!

Next morning
Guest support · 9:00 AM

Good morning James. Did the power stay on through the night? Let me know if the generator or anything else gave you trouble.

Ticket record · Closed
Issue
Area wide power outage, backup generator would not start.
Action taken
Worked out the outage was area wide, shared the backup power steps, then escalated when the generator failed.
Person contacted
Property manager and generator technician.
Resolution
Generator refuelled and running by about 8:50 PM. Guest confirmed lights and AC.
Follow up
Add a generator fuel check before every check in. Morning check in message sent.
What this shows

Local knowledge. In Lagos, power is part of the guest experience, so the playbook plans for it and the follow up fixes the cause, not just the night.
